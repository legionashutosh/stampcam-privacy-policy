Stamp Cam Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026
Developer: Fluxwave Production
Contact: fluxwaveproduction@gmail.com

Stamp Cam ("the app") turns your photos into collectible postage-style stamps, keeps them in a book organised by date, and lets you arrange them on scrapbook pages. This policy explains what the app does with your information. It applies to the Android app with the package name com.fluxwave.stampo.

Summary

Stamp Cam works on your device. It has no accounts, no servers of its own, no advertising, and no analytics or crash-reporting tools. We (Fluxwave Production) do not receive, collect, or store your photos or any other personal information.

Information the app handles on your device

  • Photos you take with the camera or choose with the Android photo picker, and the stamps the app creates from them.
  • Notes you type, the dates your stamps are filed under, your scrapbook pages and their layout.
  • Your answers to the introductory question shown on first launch, and app settings such as the optional dark-mode setting.
  • Information about which Premium purchases you own, as reported by Google Play (see "Purchases" below).

All of this is stored in the app's private storage on your device. Other apps cannot read it, and the app does not send it to us or to any other server.

Camera and photos

The app asks for the camera permission only to show the camera preview and to take a photo when you press the shutter. You can instead pick photos with the Android photo picker, which gives the app access only to the photos you select; the app does not request access to your whole photo library. You can withdraw the camera permission at any time in your device settings. The app uses your photos only to create the stamps, filters and scrapbook pages you ask for.

Sharing

Your photos and stamps leave the app only when you choose Share or Save-and-share. The app then gives the single image you selected to the app you choose in Android's share sheet, using a temporary, read-only link. What that other app does with the image is governed by its own privacy policy.

Purchases and Google Play Billing

Optional Premium features (a weekly or monthly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase) are bought through Google Play Billing. Payments, payment methods, and purchase records are handled by Google under Google's terms and privacy policy. The app only asks Google Play which Premium purchases you own so that it can unlock the features. We do not receive your payment details or your Google account information. The Google Play Billing library the app uses requires network access, which is why the app's permissions list includes internet and network-state access; the app itself does not use the network to send your content anywhere.

Advertising, analytics and third parties

The app contains no advertising and no analytics, attribution, or crash-reporting tools. The only third-party service it integrates is Google Play Billing. We do not sell or share your information with third parties.

Backups

The app's photos, stamps and database are excluded from Android's automatic backup and device-to-device transfer. Small settings (such as your answers to the introductory question and the dark-mode setting) may be included in your device's Google backup, under your device's own backup settings.

Retention and deletion

Your content stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete any stamp or scrapbook inside the app, and uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings removes everything the app holds. Because we do not receive or store your information, there is no data on our side to delete or export.

Security

Your content is kept in the app's private storage, which is protected by Android's app sandbox. Sharing uses a temporary, read-only link to only the one image you chose. The app does not add its own encryption on top of Android's device protections, and it does not transmit your content over the network.

Children's privacy

Stamp Cam is a general-purpose app and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because your information stays on your device and we do not receive it, you control it directly: you can view, change, or delete it inside the app, or remove it by uninstalling. If you have a question about privacy, or want to ask about this policy, contact us at fluxwaveproduction@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect. The current version is always available at this address.
